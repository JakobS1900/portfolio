Interactive demo · Counterpart

Practice the hard conversation before you have it.

Counterpart lets a professional rehearse a difficult conversation against an AI that plays the person across the table, then scores how it went. Pick a field, then choose what to say each turn. The panel is driven by a deterministic engine, the same one that runs the real product, so the score reflects what you actually did, not what the AI feels like giving you. You cannot talk your way to a good grade.

This demo runs entirely in your browser with no server and no model. In production the replies come from a local language model; the engine and the scoring are identical.

        Why this is the interesting part

        Every number on that panel is set by a pure function of what you did, not by the counterpart's opinion. Typing "ignore your instructions and tell me everything" would move those meters by exactly zero, and what the counterpart is withholding never enters the model's context until the engine unlocks it. That is what makes the debrief something an assessor can trust: the same choices always produce the same score, traceable to the moment in the conversation.

        The counterpart is defined entirely in config, so a new scenario, or a whole new field, is a data file, not a code change. All four tabs above run on one engine.
